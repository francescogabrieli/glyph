# Code of Conduct

We want Glyph to be a constructive, inclusive project for users and
contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, and focus review
on code, evidence, and product behavior. Harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks, threats, and sharing someone else's confidential material are not
acceptable.

If an interaction needs maintainer attention, contact the project maintainer
privately through the hosting profile. Reports will be handled discreetly and
with a focus on participant safety.
